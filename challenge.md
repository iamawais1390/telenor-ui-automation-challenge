## Telenor E2E GUI Challenge

1. Navigate to `telenor.se`.
2. Click **Handla**.
3. Click **Bredband**.
4. Enter an address: **Kungsgatan 103, Uppsala**.
5. Verify that the `featured-product-grid` is not empty.

---

## Instructions and Expectations

- The implementation should use **Playwright with JavaScript**.
- The solution must be executable in **GitHub Actions** on every push / pull request.
- It must produce **Allure reports** that can be viewed as a **GitHub Pages** site or as a **downloadable artifact**.
- You are expected to mention and practice **git branching practices**.
- Any **assumptions** you make during the solutioning of this challenge must be documented and communicated.

Looking forward to hearing from you should you have any questions.
